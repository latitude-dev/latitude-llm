# Users

The **Users** page shows the people behind your traffic. When your telemetry includes a user id, Latitude groups that user's sessions and traces together, so you can see who is affected by problems, who is most active, and how reliable their experience is.

To populate it, send a `userId` with your telemetry. See [User tracking](./features/user-tracking).

## What the Users list shows

Summary metrics across the top cover your selected time range: unique users, new users, identified sessions, and sessions per user, with a chart of successful versus errored sessions and the error rate.

Each row is one user, with:

- **email**, when provided
- **seen at**: when they were first and last seen
- a **trend** sparkline of recent activity
- **sessions**: how many sessions they had
- **errors**: their error rate and count

## Inspect a user

Open any user to see their detail view.

<Frame caption="A user's detail view: lifetime usage and cost, activity over the last 30 days, the signals affecting them, and the behaviours they show.">
  <img src="/images/users/user-detail.png" alt="A user detail view with traces, sessions, errored sessions, cost, tokens, an activity chart, the signals affecting the user, and behaviours" />
</Frame>

The detail view shows:

- **Usage and reliability**: traces, sessions, errored sessions, total cost, tokens, average trace duration, active days, and when the user was first and last seen
- **Activity** over the last 30 days, with an **Error view** to focus on failures
- **Signals affecting this user**: the tracked problems this user has run into, with status and how often (these are today's [issues](../issues/overview))
- **Behaviours**: the topics observed for this user. See [Behaviours](../search/behaviours)

## Monitor users

Use **Add monitor** on a user to watch them, or **Monitor all users** from the Users page to watch your user base as a whole.

**Monitor all users** opens a recommended set of aggregate monitors. Pick one, or switch to the **Advanced** tab to configure your own:

- **Users are having errors**: opens an incident when failed traces for users stay higher than expected.
- **Users are seeing slow responses**: opens an incident when p95 trace latency for users stays higher than expected.
- **Activity spiked**: opens an incident when trace volume for users stays higher than expected.
- **Activity dropped**: opens an incident when trace volume for users stays lower than expected.
- **Cost spiked**: opens an incident when total cost for users stays higher than expected.
